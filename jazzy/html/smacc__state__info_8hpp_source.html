31// Ownership of the introspection graph. The SmaccStateMachineInfo owns every SmaccStateInfo
32// through its `states` map; a SmaccStateInfo owns its `children_` and its `transitions_`; the
33// static maps SmaccStateInfo::stateReactorsInfo and eventGeneratorsInfo own the reactor and
34// generator infos, which own their handlers. Every pointer in the other direction (a state to its
35// machine or parent, a transition to its states, a handler to its info) is a std::weak_ptr, so the
36// graph is released when the state machine releases its SmaccStateMachineInfo. Readers of a weak
37// pointer lock it; all of them run while the state machine is alive.
38//-------------------------------------------------------------
39struct ClientBehaviorInfoEntry
40{
41 std::function<void(smacc2::ISmaccState *)> factoryFunction;
42 const std::type_info * behaviorType;
43 const std::type_info * orthogonalType;
44};
