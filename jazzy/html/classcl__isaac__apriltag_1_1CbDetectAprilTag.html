Detailed Description

Behavior that detects unvisited AprilTags and posts events.

This behavior uses requiresComponent() to access:

  • CpAprilTagTracker: For detection signals
  • CpAprilTagMissionState: For visited/selected tag management

When an unvisited tag is detected, it selects the tag and posts an EvUnvisitedAprilTagDetected event.
